Content

Home page

Fish’n’Dish is a wholesale flavor house built for chefs, restaurateurs, food manufacturers, and distributors who want to bring bold, consistent taste to their products. We’re specialists in pepper varieties, dried spices, smoked and sweet paprikas, savory blends, finishing salts, and concentrated taste boosters that amplify flavor without masking the ingredient. Our catalogue is designed around bulk-ready packaging and private-label flexibility so your brand gets the profile it needs—on the shelf or on the plate. Our core offerings include a vibrant selection of peppers — whole and ground black pepper (Tellicherry-style and standard commercial grades), white pepper, green peppercorns (freeze-dried), pink peppercorn blends, and crushed red pepper flakes — all available as coarse, cracked, or fine ground. Paprika is one of our proudest staples: sweet paprika for warmth and color, hot paprika for that lively kick, and smoked paprika for deep, cozy, barbecue-style flavor. Each comes in standard and oak-smoked profiles for that perfect pop of color and taste. We carry culinary essentials like turmeric, cumin, coriander, fennel, mustard seed, and fenugreek, plus dehydrated aromatics—onion granules, garlic powder, roasted garlic flakes—and more. At Fish’n’Dish, we like to think of our spices as little bursts of joy in a jar (or a 25-kilo bag, if that’s more your speed). We believe food should be fun, flavorful, and full of character—never bland, never boring. Whether you’re experimenting with a new blend or perfecting a family recipe that’s been around for generations, we’ve got the seasonings to make it sing. Our team of flavor enthusiasts is always happy to help you find your next favorite spice—or dream up a custom blend that’s uniquely yours. Because at the end of the day, everything tastes better when it’s made with a little curiosity, a little care, and a whole lot of flavor.

Products

Prices are wholesale, quoted per kilogram, and based on tiered discounts for larger volumes. Whole black peppercorns come at $9.50/kg, while ground black pepper is offered at $8.25/kg, sweet paprika goes for $6.50/kg, smoked paprika is at $7.25/kg, cayenne/chili powder at $5.75/kg, turmeric at $4.50/kg, cumin at $4.00/kg, coriander at $3.75/kg, garlic powder at $3.60/kg, onion granules at $3.50/kg, and specialty finishing salts go for $6.00/kg. Blends and private formulae start at $7.50/kg depending on complexity and raw ingredient cost. Concentrated bouillon bases are $12.00/kg. Pricing for packaging-sachets, bulk lined sacks, tin pails-and private labeling is extra; typical sample packs (500 g) are $15–$35 depending on the product and are waived if you place a qualifying initial order. Minimum order quantities vary by product, starting generally at 25 kg for basic spices and 100 kg for custom-blend projects; sample and small-batch trial runs can be accommodated using 5–10 kg MOQ for many items.

Pricing

All domestic U.S. orders include a flat shipping rate of $8.00 per order for standard ground delivery. Orders over $250 ship free within the continental U.S. When we ship outside of America, there’s an additional $10.00 international handling fee to cover export paperwork and customs processing. International freight rates vary by destination, but most small commercial orders average $25–$40 per kg by air or $120 per pallet by sea freight. Customers are responsible for any local import duties or taxes charged by their country. Delivery times range from 7–14 business days for domestic orders and 3–6 weeks for international shipments. s
