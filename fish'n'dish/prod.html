What We Offer

Our pepper portfolio is basically a pepper party: Whole and ground black pepper (Tellicherry-style or classic commercial grade)

White pepper (for your classy moments)

Freeze-dried green peppercorns

Pink peppercorn blends

Crushed red pepper flakes

Everything comes in coarse, cracked, or fine grind—however your taste buds like to boogie. Paprika? We practically worship paprika: Sweet paprika = warm, friendly notes

Hot paprika = fiery personality

Smoked paprika = the cozy campfire of spices

We also carry kitchen legends like turmeric, cumin, coriander, mustard seed, fennel, fenugreek, and a whole cast of dehydrated aromatics—garlic powder, roasted garlic flakes, onion granules, and more. If it smells delicious and belongs in food, odds are we’ve got it.
